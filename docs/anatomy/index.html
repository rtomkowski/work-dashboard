  1. Left sidebar
  2. 1

    Sources

    Calendars, Reminder lists, Obsidian and Jira. Toggle a source or list to show or hide it everywhere.

  3. 2

    Profile & sync status

    Pick a profile (how the page opens), open Settings, and check the four sync dots: one per source.

  4. Top of the page
  5. 3

    Greeting

    Today in one line: meetings still ahead, tasks due and what is overdue.

  6. 4

    Weather & view switcher

    Local weather, then Day, Week, Month, Board, Overdue, Statistics and Review, with counts as badges.

  7. 5

    Toolbar

    Search and commands ⌘K, step through dates, then act: Plan blocks, New note, new reminder, countdown, full screen.

  8. Agenda
  9. 6

    Day column

    Date with a bar of booked hours, so heavy days stand out.

  10. 7

    Load warnings

    Amber chips for no lunch break, long stretches without a break and overlapping events.

  11. 8

    Tasks & notes

    Reminders, Obsidian tasks and Jira issues due that day, with priority and status tags.

  12. Right panel
  13. 9

    Month & 14-day load

    Mini calendar to jump to a date, and booked hours for the next two weeks.

  14. 10

    Now / Next

    The current meeting with time left, the next one, and today's numbers.

  15. 11

    Up next & pinned notes

    The next open tasks from all sources, and notes tagged #dashboard.

  16. 12

    Deadlines, heads up & prep

    A 30-day deadline countdown, warnings for the next 7 days, and prep for the next meeting.

Work Dashboard on GitHub · Right-panel blocks can be reordered, folded or hidden; the layout is saved in the current profile.
